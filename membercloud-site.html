    Questions owners ask first.

    What kinds of businesses can use MemberCloud?

    Any business that runs memberships, plans, packages or recurring dues: dental and medical practices, mental health practices, med spas, gyms, wellness studios, veterinary clinics, clubs and local service businesses.

    How does MemberCloud streamline membership management?

    It brings plans, members, payments and reporting into one dashboard. It automates recurring charges and renewal reminders, tracks benefit usage and replaces spreadsheets and multiple apps.

    How much does MemberCloud cost?

    $149 a month (introductory pricing for a limited time). It includes unlimited admins, unlimited locations, tiered memberships and automated billing.

    Can we customize our membership plans?

    Yes. You can change plan names, pricing, billing frequency, included services, discounts and eligibility rules, and create family or multi-pet plans.

    What happens when a card fails?

    Payments are scheduled and MemberCloud follows up automatically when a card fails.

    Does MemberCloud replace our practice or booking software?

    No. It works alongside your existing practice management, booking or POS system and focuses on memberships, payments and reporting. Reports can be exported for reconciliation.

    Is there a free trial?

    Not at the moment. The free demo is a live walkthrough with our team, set up around how your business runs, so you can see your own plans before you decide. After that, MemberCloud is $149 a month.

    Where do you work?

    Anywhere in the United States. Our first clients are dental practices in Florida, and we are currently onboarding practices in South and Central Florida (East Coast), with Chicago next.

    Is it secure for a dental practice?

    Our team can confirm security and compliance details in writing during your demo.
